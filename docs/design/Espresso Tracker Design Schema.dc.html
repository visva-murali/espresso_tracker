A house style for logging shots

Mobile-first, bean-first, duplicate-and-nudge. Everything below is the contract: tokens, the parts they compose into, the rules that govern layout, and four screens built only from those parts. Build new screens by assembling this vocabulary, not by inventing next to it.

01  Principles

Four rules that settle most arguments
The number is the interface

Ratio and pull time are the two figures a shot is judged by. They set at display size, tabular, unlabelled where context is obvious. Everything else is supporting metadata at 13px.

Change, not entry

A new shot starts as a copy of the bag's reference shot. The UI's job is to show what moved: every nudged field carries a delta mark. Empty forms are the exception, not the default.

The bag is the unit

Shots are never a flat feed. They nest under a bag heading that carries roast age and dial-in state, so a row only has to state what differs from its siblings.

Drawn, not filled

Structure comes from hairlines and outlines. Gold is a stroke and a small mark, never a block of colour. No card gets a fill, no button gets a solid.

Grind is free text in the schema and stays free text in the UI — render it verbatim, right-aligned, and never coerce it to a number. Deltas are the one place a sign is shown: +0.2 and −2s read as adjustments, not as good or bad. Gold on a figure always means "this is what moved".

Bag heading

The group header for every list of shots. Grouped client-side on bean_name + roast_date — there is no bags table in v1 and this schema does not require one. Design it as if it becomes an entity: the heading is already the tappable target that a future bag screen hangs off.

Dialed Dialing Resting Past peak

State is derived, never stored: dialing until two consecutive shots land within 0.15 ratio and 2s of each other, dialed after, resting under 4 days off roast, past peak over 28. If a bag has one shot it carries no tag at all rather than a guess.

Shot row

64px minimum, hairline-separated, never boxed. Ratio and time lead at Figure L; dose, yield and grind follow at 12px because inside a bag they are usually the only things that moved. Rating renders as five marks, filled to the score, absent entirely when null. The video glyph appears only when a row has one — no empty slot.

  • Whole row is the tap target to /shots/:id. Swipe left reveals edit and delete; no per-row buttons.
  • Timestamp is weekday plus clock inside a fortnight, then a date. Never "3 days ago" — you compare mornings.
  • A signed gold delta next to a value means it differs from the previous shot on the same bag — the mark states how far it moved, not merely that it moved. Only changed fields carry one.

Nudge row

The primary input of the whole app. Value on the left at Figure L, two 48px steppers on the right where a thumb reaches. Tapping the value itself opens the keypad for a large jump; the steppers handle the ±1 increment that dialling in actually needs.

  • Steps: dose 0.1 g, yield 0.5 g, time 1s, grind 0.1 (or one notch of whatever text convention the last shot used).
  • Once a row differs from the reference shot, the value goes accent-700 and the label gains the signed delta. Untouched rows stay at full text colour — the screen reads as a diff at a glance.
  • Long-press repeats. Every stepper is idempotent against the reference, so double-back returns to the exact original value and clears the mark.

Video plate

A quiet attachment, not the hero. The pour sits matted in the system's plate treatment, below the figures on the detail screen and behind a single glyph in lists. It gets a caption line, not a chrome bar: duration, size, and a status word that Phase 2 will repurpose.

  • 16:10 mat on the detail screen; phone footage letterboxes inside it rather than cropping — the crema surface is the point.
  • Upload is a file pick, so the states are: no video (a hairline "attach pour" row), uploading (determinate rule under the caption), ready, and unplayable (the HEVC case — caption states it plainly and offers download).
  • Phase 2 hook: the caption right slot becomes the analysis state, and an overlay track can be drawn inside the same mat without changing the layout.
Inherited parts

Buttons, fields, tags, segmented controls, tables and dialogs come from the system unchanged. Do not restyle them per screen; their hover, pressed and focus states are already themed.

  • One primary per screen. On mobile it lives in the bottom bar as .btn-primary.btn-block at 48px.
  • Free-text fields (grind, tasting note) use .input. Numeric fields never do — they use nudge rows.
  • Delete is a ghost button plus the system dialog. It is the only irreversible action and never sits next to save.

05  Decisions and hooks

What this schema commits you to, and what it leaves open
QuestionDecisionConsequence in code
Bag identityClient-side grouping on bean_name + roast_date. No bags table in v1.A groupShotsByBag() helper next to listShots(); the heading is already a link target for a future /bags/:id.
Dial-in stateDerived per bag from the last two shots. Shown as a tag on the heading and as deltas on rows.Pure functions over the shots array — nothing new persisted, nothing to migrate.
Reference shotThe most recent shot on the bag, unless one is starred. Duplicate copies from it.Optional is_reference boolean on shots when you want it; until then, most recent.
Video prominenceQuiet attachment. Matted, below the figures, one glyph in lists.No layout change needed when Phase 2 adds analysis — it fills the caption's right slot and the mat's interior.
Stats scopeThree charts only: ratio × time, pull-time consistency, rating by shot on a bag.All three compute from the shots array in the client. No new tables, no aggregation service.
Colour semanticsNo good/bad colour. Gold means change: signed deltas and the values that moved. Ratios and times stay ink so the gold has something to stand against.Frees you from ever defining a "correct" ratio; the target window on the scatter is user-set, not shipped.

Adoption order that keeps the app shippable throughout: tokens and type first (a theme.css next to index.css), then the shot row and bag heading in ShotListPage, then the nudge row replacing the numeric inputs in ShotForm, then the detail hero, then trends as a new route. Nothing above requires a schema migration.
